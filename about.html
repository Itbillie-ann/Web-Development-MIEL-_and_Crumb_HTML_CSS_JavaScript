Our Story

MIEL & Crumb Bakery started as a home-baking side business before opening our small storefront on Baker Street. Since then, we've built a loyal local following purely through word-of-mouth and the smell of fresh bread drifting down the street every morning.

Our Mission

To bake genuinely fresh bread, pastries, and celebration cakes daily, using honest ingredients and friendly, personal service.

Our Vision

To become the neighbourhood's first choice for everyday baking and life's celebration cakes, both in-store and online.
