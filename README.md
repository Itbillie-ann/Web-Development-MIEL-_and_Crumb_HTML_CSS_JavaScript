## Project Overview
MIEL & Crumb Bakery is a small, independently owned retail bakery specialising in fresh bread,
pastries, and custom celebration cakes. The bakery currently has no online presence and relies
entirely on walk-in customers, phone enquiries, and word-of-mouth. This project designs and builds
a five-page brochure and lead-generation website for the bakery across three submissions:

1. **Part 1 — Building the Foundation** *(this submission)*: project proposals, research, HTML
   structure and basic content, and initial file/repository setup.
2. **Part 2 — Styling & Responsiveness**: full CSS styling and a responsive layout *(to follow)*.
3. **Part 3 — Functionality & Submission**: JavaScript functionality, form handling, testing, and
   final submission *(to follow)*.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Showcase the bakery's product range with high-quality images.
- Allow customers to browse a simple online menu with prices.
- Provide an easy way to enquire about or request custom cake orders.
- Display business information (locations, hours, contact details) clearly.
- Increase overall website traffic and reduce repetitive phone enquiries.

**Key performance indicators:**
- Number of custom cake enquiries received through the website's enquiry form each month.
- Month-on-month growth in website visits.
- A measurable drop in phone calls asking basic questions already answered on the site.

## Key Features and Functionality
- Homepage with hero banner, short introduction, and featured products.
- Menu page with a categorised, priced product listing (breads, pastries, cakes).
- Custom Cakes page with a gallery of past work and a dedicated enquiry form.
- About Us page with the bakery's story, mission, vision, and team.
- Contact page with two locations (main storefront and Saturday market stall), embedded maps,
  opening hours, and a general contact form.
